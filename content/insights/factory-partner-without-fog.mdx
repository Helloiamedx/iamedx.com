Capacity numbers are easy to print. Repeatability is harder to see. Before you trust a slide, walk the line for the SKU you actually care about — tooling condition, in-process checks, and how a failed lot gets handled when everyone is behind.

Ask who owns the borderline unit. If the answer is “it depends,” you do not yet have a partner; you have a quotation. Strong factories can show you their reject library, their rework path, and the last time they stopped a line on purpose.

Also check what sits next to your product. Shared paint lines, shared packing crews, and shared peak seasons will shape your real calendar more than the brochure’s monthly output claim.

A good factory partnership is not the lowest unit cost in week one. It is a floor you can return to with the same questions six months later and still get clear answers.
