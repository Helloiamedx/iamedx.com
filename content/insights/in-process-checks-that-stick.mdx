Most QC failures are not missing inspectors. They are checks written for engineers and ignored by the people who actually touch the part.

Translate each critical dimension into a tool, a go/no-go, or a photo pair that fits on a phone. If the check needs a laptop and a PDF, it will skip under calendar pressure.
