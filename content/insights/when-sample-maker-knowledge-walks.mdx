Approved samples hide hand tricks. A soft wipe, a second pass of edge paint, a packing sequence that only one person remembers — none of that shows in the STEP file.

Before volume, ask who can rebuild the sample without the original maker in the room. If the answer is one name, you do not have a process yet.
