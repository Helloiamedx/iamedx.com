Open tooling loves unfinished debates. Colorways, wall thickness, insert geometry, and lid snap all feel reversible until steel is cut.

Write a freeze date and what is allowed to move after it. Cosmetic swaps can stay soft. Anything that breaks a cavity should be treated like a new program.
