A brand system that only works in Figma will not survive finishing tolerances. Edge quality, color drift, soft-touch wear, and packaging crush all rewrite the story the customer actually receives.

Translate intent into measurable ranges early. If “premium” means a specific edge radius, say so. If the logo must stay sharp after pad print at volume, put the reject example next to the approved pull. Factories protect what they can measure.

The brand decision that lasts is the one that still looks true on a dusty carton in the warehouse — not only on a hero shot. Product strategy and manufacturing reality are not separate chapters; they are the same page read at two distances.
